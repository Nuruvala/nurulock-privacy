# NuruLock privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-13.

NuruLock collects no personal data, shares no data, and cannot send data anywhere: the app has no internet permission.

## What the app uses, and why

- **Accessibility service.** NuruLock uses Android's Accessibility API to detect volume key presses (to count lock and unlock sequences), to draw the touch shield and floating bubble over other apps, and to close the notification shade while the screen is locked. For the last, it is notified only when Android's own system UI changes. It does not read screen content, text you type, or which apps you use.

- **Vibration**, for feedback when you press keys or lock and unlock.

- **Google Play Billing**, for the one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). NuruLock only learns whether the purchase is owned, and stores that on your device.

Your settings (triggers, press count, bubble look) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
